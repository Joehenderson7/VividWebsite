Central 70 Project

Process control and field & laboratory materials testing for the transformation of I-70 through Denver.

Project Overview

Modernizing a critical Denver corridor.

Vivid Engineering, working alongside Kiewit Infrastructure Co., provided essential services for the Central 70 Project. Our testing encompassed soils, concrete, asphalt, masonry, shotcrete, and aggregates to ensure the project met the Colorado Department of Transportation's rigorous quality standards.

The project modernizes a 10-mile stretch of I-70 between Brighton Boulevard and Chambers Road — a key corridor supporting 1,200 businesses and carrying over 200,000 vehicles daily. Work included new express lanes, removal of a 56-year-old viaduct, lowering the interstate between Brighton and Colorado boulevards, and creating a 4-acre park over part of the highway.
