The People Behind The Projects

Talented engineers, inspectors, technicians, and project managers dedicated to delivering exceptional quality and precision on every project.

Leadership

Experienced. Committed. Client-focused.

Our leadership team is composed of highly experienced professionals dedicated to delivering top-quality engineering and construction services. With decades of expertise across transportation, energy, water, and municipal projects, our team leads with a commitment to innovation, client satisfaction, and operational excellence.

Each leader brings unique strengths — from strategic planning and project management to quality assurance and environmental compliance — ensuring the success of every project we undertake.

William "Bill" Barreire, PE
President & CEO

Bill brings over 24 years of geotechnical engineering experience spanning construction inspection, materials testing, and environmental services across the transportation, energy, and utility sectors. A Colorado-registered Professional Engineer, he has directed everything from modest commercial projects to multi-billion-dollar transportation programs.
Steven Jeffords
Vice President & CFO

With more than three decades of experience, Steven was instrumental in establishing Vivid as a company in 2016, building its banking, accounting, HR, and insurance foundations. He has managed programs valued up to $250 million and engineering operations of more than 130 personnel across the transportation, municipal, water, and private sectors.
Danny Bennett
Vice President & COO

Danny holds 18 years of heavy civil construction and engineering consulting experience, delivering construction inspection across the water, energy, commercial, and government sectors. His career highlights include CDOT's T-REX and MoDOT's I-29/35 design-builds, and directing roughly 60 professionals on the $2.1B EAGLE P3 commuter rail project.
Derek Lindvall, PE
Vice President

Derek brings 27 years of engineering experience and oversees Vivid's CDOT and FHWA Central Federal Lands programs, managing transportation projects valued from under $1 million to over $100 million. He currently serves as Southern Colorado Director on ACEC Colorado's Board of Directors.
