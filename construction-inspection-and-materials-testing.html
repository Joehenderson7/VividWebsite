Construction Inspection & Testing

Comprehensive construction inspection, quality assurance, and materials testing — expert oversight from start to finish for CDOT, municipal, and commercial projects.

Quality & Compliance

Expert oversight from start to finish.

Vivid Engineering delivers comprehensive construction inspection and testing services focused on quality and compliance. Our team specializes in construction administration, quality control, and quality assurance — including owner’s acceptance programs for CDOT and municipal work — so your project meets every regulatory and safety requirement.

From daily field inspection and testing to supplier and plant audits, we provide accurate, reliable results that keep your project on track, with experience on major corridors like the I-25 South Gap, Central 70, SH 7, and CO 119.
