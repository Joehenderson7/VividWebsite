Environmental Services

Expert guidance and solutions to navigate complex environmental regulations and ensure compliance — from site investigations to regulatory reporting.

Compliance & Protection

Clear answers in a complex regulatory landscape.

Vivid Engineering’s environmental practice delivers expert guidance and solutions to navigate complex environmental regulations and ensure compliance. We offer a full range of investigative and advisory services — from Phase I and II environmental site investigations and CDOT initial site assessments to hazardous building materials surveys.

When issues are found, we provide tailored remediation recommendations, and our commitment to erosion control and stormwater quality helps keep your site — and the environment around it — protected from start to finish.
