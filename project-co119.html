CO 119 Safety, Mobility & Bikeway Project

Field and laboratory materials testing for the transformation of the Diagonal Highway between Boulder and Longmont.

Project Overview

Reconnecting Boulder County's busiest commuter corridor.

Vivid Engineering Group is providing field and laboratory materials testing across both construction packages (CP1 and CP2) of the CO 119 Safety, Mobility & Bikeway Project, a $165 million transformation of the Diagonal Highway between Boulder and Longmont. Working alongside Kraemer North America and the Colorado Department of Transportation, our testers sample and test soils, aggregate base course, concrete paving (PCCP), structural concrete for bridge decks, caissons, and drainage structures, and asphalt to verify every material placed meets CDOT specifications.

The project adds a separated commuter bikeway running the length of the corridor — with four underpasses at CO 119, Niwot Road, CO 52, and Jay Road, and an overpass at 63rd Street — plus new Bus Rapid Transit infrastructure, Park-n-Ride facilities, a reconfigured CO 52 intersection, and safety improvements at intersections along the corridor. Our team has been on site since 2024, supporting early-morning fast-track paving pours, bridge deck placements, caisson pours, and embankment work to keep this fast-moving corridor project on schedule.
