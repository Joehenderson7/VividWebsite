Geotechnical & Geological Engineering

Expert solutions for complex subsurface conditions — from investigations and lab testing to foundation, pavement, and slope designs, delivered by licensed Colorado Professional Engineers.

Subsurface Expertise

Reliable engineering from the ground down.

Vivid Engineering’s geotechnical and geological engineering practice provides expert solutions for complex subsurface conditions. From comprehensive subsurface investigations and geotechnical lab testing to specialized designs for earthwork, foundations, floor systems, and pavements, we deliver reliable, innovative engineering matched to each project’s unique needs.

Our team specializes in slope and rock stability, landslide and rockfall mitigation, and forensic evaluations — ensuring safety and stability in every design. Whether it’s a small-scale development or major infrastructure like the Cheyenne Canyon bridges or the Garden of the Gods pedestrian bridge, Vivid is your trusted partner for geotechnical excellence across Colorado.
