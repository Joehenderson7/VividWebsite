Denver, CO · Full-Time · $20–$40/hr

Engineering Materials Technician

Experienced concrete, soils, or asphalt testing & inspection. Perform field and lab testing of concrete, soils, aggregates, and asphalt; inspect embankments, utilities, structural concrete, and traffic control; drilled shaft inspections, quantity tracking, and daily field reporting. Up to 25% travel; positions also available in Colorado Springs and Pueblo.

Qualifications: 1+ year of experience and a valid driver's license. ACI Field/Strength/Aggregates, WAQTC Soils, CAPA, ICC Special Inspection, or CDOT certifications preferred.

Benefits: 401(k) with matching, health, dental, vision & life insurance, HSA, paid time off, on-the-job training, and cell phone reimbursement.

Colorado Springs, CO · Full-Time · $20–$40/hr

Inspector / Tester — Soils, Concrete & Asphalt

CDOT construction projects. Perform routine inspection and testing of concrete, soils, masonry, and asphalt to verify compliance with plans and specifications — soil classification, sieve analysis, gradation, moisture content — and prepare daily field reports. 8-hour shifts with weekends as needed.

Qualifications: One or more of WAQTC, ACI Field Level 1 / Lab / Strength, or CAPA A/B/C/E/I certifications; CDOT Plan Reading, Math, Survey & ATSER; clean driving record; able to pass drug test and UPRR/BNSF/DIA background checks. 1 year of CDOT construction experience preferred.

Benefits: 401(k), health, dental & vision insurance, paid time off, and opportunities for advancement.
