Laboratory Materials Testing

AASHTO-accredited laboratories in Colorado Springs, Denver, and Pueblo West — testing soils, aggregates, concrete, masonry, and asphalt under the supervision of licensed Colorado Professional Engineers.

Accredited Facilities

Three accredited labs. One standard of quality.

Vivid Engineering Group operates full-service construction materials testing laboratories in Colorado Springs, Denver, and Pueblo West, all accredited by the American Association of State Highway and Transportation Officials (AASHTO). Our facilities participate in the AASHTO R-18 accreditation program and comply with ASTM E-329-20, and every laboratory operation is supervised by a licensed Colorado Professional Engineer.

From routine compaction and compressive-strength testing to mix designs and specialty soils work, our labs support owner acceptance (OA) testing, QA/QC and process control programs, geotechnical investigations, and forensic evaluations for CDOT, municipal, federal, energy, and commercial projects across Colorado.
