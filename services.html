Our Services

Expert geotechnical, testing, and engineering services across transportation, government, energy, and commercial markets — delivered by a CDOT-certified Emerging Small Business.

SVC / GEO

Geotechnical / Geological Engineering

Our Geotechnical and Geological Engineering services provide expert solutions for complex subsurface conditions — from comprehensive subsurface investigations and lab testing to specialized designs for earthwork, foundations, pavements, drainage, and walls.

  • Subsurface investigations & exploratory drilling
  • Foundation, earthwork & retaining wall design
  • Slope stability & geohazard evaluation
  • Pavement design recommendations
SVC / CIT

Construction Inspection & Testing

Comprehensive construction inspection and testing services to ensure your project meets the highest standards of quality and compliance. Our team specializes in construction administration, quality control, and owner's acceptance programs for CDOT and municipal work.

  • Owner's Acceptance inspection & materials testing
  • Process control & QA/QC programs
  • Construction administration
  • Drainage, wall, bridge & pavement inspection
SVC / ENV

Environmental Services

Expert guidance and solutions to navigate complex environmental regulations and ensure compliance — a full range of services from site investigations to regulatory reporting.

  • Phase I & II Environmental Site Investigations
  • Asbestos & lead-based paint inspections
  • Hazardous materials surveys
  • Environmental audits, regulatory reporting & erosion control
SVC / LAB

Laboratory Materials Testing

Our Colorado Springs and Denver laboratories are accredited by the American Association of State Highway and Transportation Officials (AASHTO) and equipped to handle a wide range of testing — ensuring projects meet the highest standards of quality and safety.

  • Soils & aggregates
  • Concrete, masonry & shotcrete
  • Asphalt
  • AASHTO-accredited facilities in two cities
