Our Services

Geotechnical engineering, construction materials testing, inspection, and environmental services across transportation, government, energy, and commercial markets — delivered by a CDOT-certified Emerging Small Business.

SVC / GEO

Geotechnical / Geological Engineering

Our Geotechnical and Geological Engineering services provide expert solutions for complex subsurface conditions — from comprehensive subsurface investigations and lab testing to specialized designs for earthwork, foundations, pavements, drainage, and walls.

  • Subsurface investigations & exploratory drilling
  • Foundation, earthwork & retaining wall design
  • Slope stability & geohazard evaluation
  • Pavement design recommendations

Construction Inspection & Testing
