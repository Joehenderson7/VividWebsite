I-70 Floyd Hill Project

Construction inspection on CDOT's $905 million rebuild of eight miles of I-70 from Floyd Hill to the Veterans Memorial Tunnels.

Project Overview

Rebuilding the mountain corridor's toughest eight miles.

Where I-70 drops off Floyd Hill and threads Clear Creek Canyon to the Veterans Memorial Tunnels, the interstate narrows, curves, and climbs some of the steepest grades on the mountain corridor — a bottleneck for ski traffic, freight, and every trip between Denver and the high country. CDOT's $905 million I-70 Floyd Hill Project is rebuilding eight miles of the corridor (MP 241–248) through Clear Creek County: adding a third westbound lane that will operate as a tolled Express Lane, realigning the highway for better sight distance, replacing worn bridges — including an elevated section carrying westbound traffic through the canyon — building a two-mile frontage road between the US 6 and Hidden Valley interchanges, adding roundabouts at three interchange locations, and improving the Clear Creek Greenway below. Design-build contractor Kraemer North America broke ground in 2023, with westbound traffic slated for its new alignment in 2027 and full completion in 2029.

Working directly for CDOT, Vivid Engineering Group provides construction inspection on the project — verifying that the work going in the ground matches the plans and specifications on one of the most complex active corridors in the state. Vivid inspectors cover the full range of heavy-civil operations: drilled shafts and pier columns rising out of the canyon floor, girder erection and bridge deck placements, rock excavation and retaining walls, earthwork, paving, and drainage — all built under live interstate traffic in a narrow canyon shared with Clear Creek and the greenway. It's exactly the kind of high-stakes, high-country work Vivid's inspection team was built for.
